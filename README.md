### Here's what drives me:

- ☁️ **Cloud done properly:** At Eywa Solutions I migrated workloads to AWS and provisioned infrastructure with Terraform and CloudFormation. At CTR Hotels I ran the cloud behind reservation, CRM and mobile platforms.
- 🎓 **Sharing what I learn:** I co-founded the AWS Student Committee in Mumbai, where we taught 200+ students the fundamentals of cloud and mentored 20+ through their AWS certifications.
- 🌍 **Mumbai to New York:** My career runs from one city to the other, and my website tells that story as a ride you can take.

### 🚀 Featured project: [dhruvpatel78.com](https://dhruvpatel78.com)

My resume as a 3D ride, and my take on the [Cloud Resume Challenge](https://cloudresumechallenge.dev/). Scroll and a character travels through my career, from school in Mumbai to work in New York, changing vehicles and crossing the ocean by plane on the way. On a desktop you can leave the road and explore the world freely.

- 🎨 **Front end:** plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with Three.js. No framework, no build step, and every model and sound is generated in code.
- 🌐 **Hosting:** S3 behind CloudFront, with ACM and Route 53.
- 🔢 **Back end:** a view counter on Lambda and DynamoDB.
- 🛠️ **Delivery:** Terraform for all of it, deployed by GitHub Actions.
